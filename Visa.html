Pakistan Tourist Visa Information

Pakistan welcomes visitors from around the world to explore its breathtaking landscapes, rich cultural heritage, historical landmarks, and warm hospitality. To enter Pakistan, most foreign nationals require a valid visa.

Types of Tourist Visas

  • Tourist Visa: For leisure travel, sightseeing, and visiting friends or family.
  • Visa on Arrival: Available to citizens of selected countries.
  • Electronic Visa (e-Visa): Apply online without visiting an embassy.

Basic Requirements

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Travel itinerary or hotel booking
  • Proof of sufficient funds
  • Return or onward travel ticket
